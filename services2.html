01DRY CARGO

Our dry cargo service is the simplest and most common one we offer. It has been developed to ensure the safe and efficient shipment of any containerised cargo.

For most of our customers, this service provides the support and equipment to move some of the world’s most commonly traded commodities such as paper, white-goods and scrap metals; however we understand that not all cargoes are quite this straightforward.

During our over a decade of experience, we’ve learned how to safely handle, stow and transport more complex cargoes, in turn allowing us to extend our dry cargo services to customers looking to ship food-grade, hazardous and rare/precious cargoes.

  • Customized freight solutions at competitive pricing
  • Timely alerts and notices with track-and-trace monitoring
  • ISF filing and full customs clearance
  • Local trucking and door-to-door delivery
  • Storage and warehouse options
  • Complete purchase order management
