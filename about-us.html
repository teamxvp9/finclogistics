01Finnish Couriers LOGISTICS

One of the leaders in the security haulage, bullion logistics and more

Providing a comprehensive range of world class services, underpinned by our dedication and professionalism, we have continued to grow, establishing Finnish Couriers AND LOGISTICS as the leading provider of security logistics, bullion management services and solutions.
We are a customer centric organisation focused on understanding our customers’ needs and delivering tailored solutions to match. This commitment drives every aspect of our business from service design to performance, guaranteeing continual development and customer satisfaction. Finnish Couriers AND LOGISTICS has a corporate vision and focused values to ensure our strategy is well presented; our mission is to provide the highest standards of service, delivering value, quality and innovation to all customers – this is underpinned by our corporate values: Integrity, Expertise, Customer Focus, Innovation, Best Service and Respect.

At Finnish Couriers AND LOGISTICS, we pride ourselves in our ability to provide a personalized security & logistics service to our valued clients simultenausly. We are an experienced industry expert who has proudly served multinational clients since 2004, MFSL delivers first class professional services to our valued clients within the public and private sectors.

Guided by our three founding fundamentals: Principle, Diligence and Service, GSL strives to provide the highest standard of premium quality and care. Our specialized team of well-trained professionals are handpicked from both active and retired law enforcement, financial experts and transportation officers with years of experience. This knowledge and expertise enables us to personally select the right security and customer service experts that are tailored fit for our client’s needs

Our active, strong and long-term relationships with business and community leaders assures that we deeply understand the important issues facing the logistics and security industries. Whether Security Logistics Delivery, Cash in Transit Services, execurity package delivery or basic package logistics, Finnish Couriers & LOGISTICS is equipped to supply innovative and individual solutions to your service needs.
Our dedicated team is ready 24/7 to fulfill your important requests and we always aim to exceed all expectations.
